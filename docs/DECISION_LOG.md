# Decision Log

**Append-only.** One row per non-obvious choice, with its reason.

Rules:

1. **Never edit or delete a past row.** If a decision changes, add a new row that supersedes it and
   mark the old one `superseded by D-0NN`. The record of what we believed and when is the point.
2. Every row needs a **reason**, not just a choice. "Because it's better" is not a reason.
3. If a decision invalidates existing results, say which ones in the row.
4. Add the row **when you make the choice**, not when you remember to. This file writes half the
   method document; that only works if it is written contemporaneously.

Owner tags: **S** filter core · **M** learning · **D** data/eval · **P** maps · **A** Android · **C** submission.

| D-028 | 2026-08-26 | The filter carries the **right-invariant** error `η = X̂X⁻¹`. GNSS, which is a left-invariant observation, is applied through the adjoint rather than the covariance being carried in its convention. | The always-on measurement family — NHC, ZUPT, ZARU, learned speed — is body-frame, which pairs with the right-invariant error; GNSS is optional and χ²-gated (D-001) and absent for the 10–180 s windows we are graded on. Verified consequence: the body-frame velocity Jacobian has an **exactly zero attitude column**, so the filter cannot acquire the spurious yaw observability that makes a naive EKF inconsistent. | D-029 | 2026-08-26 | Nominal propagation uses the exact Γ₀/Γ₁/Γ₂ closed form, not a first-order Euler step; and `A_RI` is evaluated at the step **midpoint**. | At 10 Hz and 0.6 rad/s the Γ₁ correction is ~3% of the velocity increment and is rotation-direction-dependent, so it does not average out over the 1800 steps of a 180 s outage — it is a systematic heading-correlated drift, the error class the budget has no room for. Midpoint evaluation of `A` measured ~1800× lower linearisation residual at Δt = 1 ms. Both cost a handful of flops. | S |
| D-030 | 2026-08-26 | Mount-error perturbation convention is `R̂_sv = Exp(ξ_sv) R_sv` (vehicle-frame, left multiplication). | Makes `ξ_sv,z` literally mount-yaw error in the vehicle frame, so its covariance diagonal is directly comparable to the ~1° requirement in [ERROR_BUDGET.md](ERROR_BUDGET.md) §5 without a change of basis. A right-multiplied convention would have needed a rotation applied every time the budget was checked, which is a step someone eventually skips. | S |
| D-031 | 2026-08-26 | Discrete process noise `Q_d` by Van Loan matrix-exponential, not the `Φ G Q_c Gᵀ Φᵀ Δt` approximation. | Measured 4.2% error at Δt = 0.1 s with our own `FilterConfig` noise values. It mis-allocates noise between the position and velocity blocks, and an under-sized Q makes the filter reject good measurements at the χ² gate — a failure that looks like a sensor problem, not a tuning problem. Cost is one 36×36 `expm` per step, affordable in the Python reference; the October C++ port must validate any cheaper closed form against it. | S |
| D-032 | 2026-08-26 | Small-angle cutoff for the Γ functions is **1e-3**, not the 1e-6 first written down. | The guard must sit where the closed form is still accurate, not where it divides by zero. Sweeping the exact identities `Γ₀ = I + φ^∧Γ₁` / `Γ₁ = I + φ^∧Γ₂` puts the worst residual at φ ≈ 1.2e-6 — inside the branch a 1e-6 cutoff sends to the closed form, where `φ²+2cosφ−2` keeps almost no significant figures. Found by writing the test, not by reading the formula. | S |
| D-033 | 2026-08-26 | The SE₂(3) derivation is checked in CI ([tests/test_se23_derivation.py](../tests/test_se23_derivation.py)) against numerical ground truth, before the filter that uses it exists. | A wrong matrix on paper becomes a filter that runs, produces smooth plausible trajectories, and is undebuggable — and Gate 1 is a hard stop with no retry. Three corrections (D-029 midpoint, D-031 magnitude, D-032 cutoff) came out of writing the checks rather than out of reading the algebra, which is the argument for doing it in this order. | S |

| ID | Date | Decision | Reason | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-034 | 2026-08-26 | The NCR extract merges **both** Geofabrik `northern-zone` and `central-zone` before clipping. | Verified by point-in-polygon against Geofabrik's published `.poly` boundaries: Delhi, Gurugram, Faridabad, Sonipat and Alwar are northern; Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad and Meerut are central. The seam is the Yamuna, so a northern-only graph loses every eastern crossing — DND Flyway, Akshardham, Kalindi Kunj — and the whole Noida–Ghaziabad side. A matcher whose graph ends mid-bridge does not degrade gracefully; it snaps to the nearest thing still in the graph. | P |
| D-035 | 2026-08-26 | Candidate search uses the **Mahalanobis distance against the filter's own `P_pos`**, not a scalar radius. | The 99% radius implied by our error budget runs from 18 m at a 10 s outage to 359 m at 180 s — a 20× span across the outage lengths the protocol mandates. Set it for 180 s and every parallel service road and flyover deck within 360 m enters the candidate set at 10 s; set it for 10 s and the true road is absent at 180 s. The terms are also anisotropic — at 60 s along-track error is 1.5× the combined lateral, and the ellipse rotates with heading — so a scalar σ is a circle fitted to a rotating ellipse. `d_M² = δᵀ P_pos⁻¹ δ` costs one 2×2 solve per candidate and is simply the correct quantity. | P |
| D-036 | 2026-08-26 | **Build the HMM matcher over our own CSR graph; do not adopt a library.** | Every candidate library takes GPS accuracy as one scalar — Valhalla Meili precomputes `1/(2·σ_z²)` with `σ_z` defaulting to 4.07 m, and FMM's `gps_error`, GraphHopper's `measurement_error_sigma` and Barefoot's `sigma` are the same shape. That is the right model for raw GPS traces and the wrong one for a filtered pose whose covariance we compute. Adopting one means either discarding D-015/D-035 or patching someone else's core probability model. Viterbi over the CSR is ~200 lines, and the CSR is ours regardless, because its mmap behaviour is what makes matching affordable on Android. | P |
| D-037 | 2026-08-26 | `highway=service` **is** included in the drivable graph, with highway class carried in a 4-bit edge flag as an emission prior. | Measured cost is +21% (57,922 service ways against 280,884 drivable), not the assumed 2×, so the memory objection collapses. Excluding forces a car genuinely on a service lane — one runs beside every Delhi arterial — onto the arterial. Including keeps it reachable but makes it out-argue a trunk road to win. | P |

| ID | Date | Decision | Reason | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-045 | 2026-08-28 | **`Q_c` is seeded from the measured values `gyro_arw = 4.11e-4` rad/s/√Hz (1.41 °/√hr), `accel_vrw = 7.46e-3` m/s²/√Hz (0.45 m/s/√hr), with `gyro_bias_rw = 5.48e-5` rad/s²/√Hz and `accel_bias_rw = 1.04e-3` m/s³/√Hz *derived* rather than measured.** Measured bias instability: gyro 42 °/hr at τ ≈ 45 s, accel 0.34 mg at τ ≈ 20 s. Segments: `S-T2[31422:36490]` (507 s) and `S-T7[47809:52285]` (448 s), overlapping Allan deviation per axis at 10 Hz, worst axis taken. Tool: [`eval/allan.py`](../eval/allan.py); artefacts in `eval/figures/allan_*.csv`, stamped. Executes R-1 and closes R-2. | Four things, in order of how much they change the answer. (1) **The premise was wrong.** [DATASETS.md](DATASETS.md) §1.4 promised ">20 min" stationary segments; sweeping all 168 distinct `S-` files, the longest continuous, uniformly-sampled, still stretch is 507 s. τ_max is therefore ~51 s at the T/10 confidence limit, not the 100–200 s the plan assumed — so bias instability is an upper bound (the curve is still descending at τ_max on most axes) and the +½ rate-random-walk slope is unreachable. The bias driving noises come instead from a first-order Gauss–Markov model on the two measured quantities, `q = B√(2/τ_c)`, and are labelled as derived everywhere they appear. (2) **Stationary is not the same as quiet.** `S-A6`'s segments pass the ZUPT detector and carry 20–30× the mechanical energy of the `S-T` ones, returning an apparent ARW ~6× larger. That measures the cabin, not the gyroscope; segments must sit an order of magnitude inside the ZUPT thresholds to characterise a sensor, and in-cabin vibration becomes an explicit Q inflation term at Gate 1 instead. (3) **A coefficient is only an ARW if the band is white.** Five of six accelerometer fits failed a ±0.15 check on the −½ slope — a parked car really does accelerate at low frequency — so VRW rests on one axis (`accel_z`, S-T7) and says so rather than averaging three numbers of which two are not VRWs. (4) The result, 1.41 °/√hr, sits inside the 0.5–5 °/√hr phone range our own budget states, replacing a 3.0e-3 placeholder that was 10.3 °/√hr — 7× pessimistic and outside that range (R-2, self-flagged in [SE23_PROPAGATION.md](SE23_PROPAGATION.md) §10). | S |
| D-046 | 2026-08-28 | **Bias instability is `B = σ_min / 0.664`, a division.** [ERROR_BUDGET.md](ERROR_BUDGET.md) §9's earlier "the flat minimum gives bias instability (×0.664)" is corrected in place, and `eval.allan.BIAS_INSTABILITY_COEFF` is defined as `√(2 ln2 / π)` with a test pinning both the constant and the direction. | IEEE Std 952's flicker-floor relation is `σ(τ) = B √(2 ln2/π) = 0.664 B`, so the Allan minimum is 0.664 *of* B. Multiplying instead of dividing understates B by 2.27×, and it understates it in the dangerous direction: the filter would believe a drifting gyro bias more than it should, exactly where the error budget has least room (lateral error is 40 m of a 100 m budget and is gyro-bias-driven). The phrasing was ambiguous rather than plainly wrong, which is why it survived a review — hence the test rather than only a doc fix. | S |
| D-047 | 2026-08-28 | **The `S-` loader's column allowlist gains explicit aliases for the header spellings IO-VNBD actually ships, and reads the files as latin-1.** Guard semantics are unchanged: the aliases map shipped spellings onto names already on the allowlist and add no new permitted concept. | The allowlist was written against the paper's column names, not the CSVs, and no code in the repo had ever loaded the dataset — so this was invisible until R-1 needed real bytes. Every real `S-` file was rejected wholesale: `ACCELEROMETER X (m/s²)` normalises generically to `accelerometer_x`, which is not on the allowlist, and all three `ORIENTATION (Yaw\|Pitch\|Roll)` columns collapse to the single name `orientation` once parenthesised groups are stripped. The headers also carry raw 0xB0/0xB5 bytes, so the default utf-8 read raises before the guard runs at all. Two further spellings ship (`GYROSCOPE X/Y/Z` for `Yaw/Pitch/Roll` — verified byte-identical on `S-S1`, which appears in both folders, so this is AndroSensor labelling and not a different axis order; and `SATELLITES IN RANGE` without the `GPS` prefix). The `time since start` alias is anchored on its `(ms` unit specifically so the `V-` stream's `Time Since Start of Day (seconds)` cannot be laundered into an allowed name; a test asserts that it is not. A guard that rejects everything is not safer than one that works — it is untested, and it would have been "fixed" under deadline pressure by someone switching the allowlist off. | S |

## Sprint 3 — P-11, `R_sv` in-filter, the PCA initialiser and the bump detector (1 Sep 2026)

| ID | Date | Decision | Reason | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-073 | 2026-09-01 | **`pca_mount_yaw` initialises `R_sv` from horizontal specific force and returns the *spread* alongside the angle; `initial_covariance` takes that spread for `P₀`'s mount block.** Spread is `√(λ₁λ₂ / (n_eff(λ₁−λ₂)²))` with `n_eff = n(1−ρ)/(1+ρ)` from the measured lag-1 autocorrelation. | An initialiser that returns only an angle forces the caller to invent an uncertainty for it, which is exactly the hole D-055 had to fill with a stated 5°. The formula is the asymptotic standard error of a 2D principal-axis angle and it is **checked by Monte Carlo** rather than recalled — within 7% over eigenvalue ratios 2–25 and n from 200 to 5000. `n_eff` rather than `n` because 10 Hz accelerometer samples are strongly serially correlated and using the sample count understates the spread by the square root of the correlation time; the AR(1) correction is derived, and labelled as derived, in the spirit of D-045's Gauss–Markov bias noises. | S |
| D-074 | 2026-09-01 | **PCA's 180° ambiguity is reported, never guessed.** `MountInit.sign_resolved` is False unless a `forward_reference` — a per-sample signed scalar that grows with forward acceleration, e.g. finite-differenced `gps_speed_kmh` — is supplied to break it. | A covariance cannot distinguish forward from backward: they share a principal component exactly. A 180° mount error is not a large version of a small one — it is a vehicle driving backwards — and it will **not** converge out under NHC, whose lateral and vertical rows are satisfied just as well by a reversed forward axis. So it must be resolved by something outside the covariance or declared unresolved. Guessing it would be the single most expensive silent error the initialiser could make. | S |
| D-075 | 2026-09-01 | **`mount_disturbance_gyro_thresh = 3.0` rad/s cannot detect the 5° knock the detector exists for, and the threshold is NOT lowered to make a test pass. Recorded as an open gap.** | Closed-form: a knock of angle θ delivered inside one sample presents at most `θ/Δt` of measured rate, because the stream is 10 Hz and the transient is averaged away below Nyquist. So 3.0 rad/s corresponds to `3.0 × 0.1 = 0.3` rad = **17.2°**, while [ERROR_BUDGET.md](ERROR_BUDGET.md) §5's 5° knock presents 0.87 rad/s — measured at 0.875 in the P-11 scenario, and the detector does not fire. Lowering the threshold until the synthetic knock trips it would be fitting a detector to a scenario, and the threshold has no source in this repo to fit it to instead. What it needs is a recording of a real phone being knocked in a cradle, which is ten minutes of seat A's time and does not touch the Gate 1 critical path. Until then: the re-inflation mechanism is exercised by signalling the knock directly, the detector guards only a violent one, and the ~1° requirement in §5 rests on the PCA initialiser plus unaided NHC. A test asserts the blind spot and its arithmetic, so resolving it will fail loudly rather than quietly. | S |
| D-076 | 2026-09-01 | **`reinflate_mount` *adds* variance to the mount block and leaves the rotation alone.** Default 5°, from §5. | Two choices in one line, both the safe direction. **Adds** rather than resets: a reset to a fixed value would *shrink* the covariance of a filter that had already lost track, and that is the direction from which nothing recovers. **Leaves the state alone** because a knock is news about uncertainty, not a measurement of a new angle — the gyro energy that detected it does not say how far the phone turned. NHC then re-estimates `R_sv` from the widened prior with gain equal to forward speed (§7.2), which is why this works on a motorway and barely at all in a car park. Measured over 60 s at 15 m/s after a 5° knock: 0.43° residual with re-inflation against 8.56° without — worse than the knock, because a stale mount behind a confident covariance drags the rest of the state with it. | S |
